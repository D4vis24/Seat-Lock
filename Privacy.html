Privacy Policy

SeatLock - Airline Interview Prep

Last Updated: September 30, 2026

Introduction

SeatLock ("the App", "we") helps pilots prepare for airline interviews. This policy explains what the App stores, what leaves your device, and the choices you have.

🛡️ Privacy at a Glance

  • Your practice history, scores, and recordings are stored on your device. SeatLock has no accounts and no database of users.
  • AI feedback is optional. If you allow it, the text of your answer is sent through SeatLock's server to Anthropic's Claude AI to grade it. Your voice recordings are not sent.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, use third-party analytics, or track you across apps and websites.

Information Stored on Your Device

  • Profile and preferences: target airline, interview date, daily goal, the first name you enter (used only for spoken greetings), and voice settings.
  • Practice data: your recorded answers, their transcripts, scores, feedback, progress, streaks, achievements, and saved tips.

This data is stored only on your device. It isn't sent to us, and it isn't synced to iCloud by the App.

Information That Leaves Your Device

AI feedback and Ask George (only if you allow AI feedback)

The App asks for your permission before it sends anything to an AI service. If you allow it, each AI-graded answer sends:

  • the text transcript of your answer and how long you spoke;
  • the question, its category, and reference material for that question;
  • your target airline and its published values;
  • a summary of your recent practice (average score, number of sessions, trend, and recent improvement notes).

Ask George sends the question you type or speak and your target airline.

This information is sent over HTTPS to SeatLock's server (hosted on Cloudflare), which forwards it to Anthropic's Claude API and returns the feedback. Your name, email, contact details, and voice recordings are not sent.

You can turn AI feedback off at any time in Settings > AI Features. When it's off, answers get an estimate calculated on your device and nothing is sent to an AI service.

Anonymous device identifier

AI grading requests include Apple's anonymous app-specific device identifier (identifierForVendor) so the server can enforce daily usage limits. The server stores a count of AI grades for that identifier and deletes it automatically within 2 days. The identifier can't be used to identify you and isn't linked to your name, Apple ID, or answers.

Speech recognition (Apple)

Turning your spoken answers into text uses Apple's Speech framework. Depending on your device and settings, Apple may process the audio on its servers. Apple's handling is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Question audio (OpenAI)

When the natural "Alex" voice reads a question aloud, the question's text is sent through SeatLock's server to OpenAI's text-to-speech API. Only the question text is sent, never your answers or personal information. Generated audio is cached on your device. The optional voice pack downloads from our content server (Cloudflare R2).

Purchases (Apple)

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment information.

Notifications

Practice reminders are scheduled on your device. No notification data is sent to us.

What Our Server Keeps

SeatLock's server does not store your answers, transcripts, or questions. It passes each request to the AI provider and returns the result. It keeps only:

  • the daily AI-grade count per anonymous device identifier, deleted within 2 days;
  • short-lived request counts per IP address, held in memory to prevent abuse.

Cloudflare, which hosts the server, may process IP addresses and request metadata for security and operations under Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

Data Sharing

We do NOT:

  • Sell or rent your information
  • Share it for advertising or marketing
  • Use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs
  • Use your answers for anything other than giving you feedback

Your Choices

  • AI feedback: turn it on or off in Settings > AI Features.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: allow or deny in the iOS Settings app.
  • Notifications: turn them off in the iOS Settings app.
  • Delete your data: "Reset All Progress" in Settings deletes your saved practice sessions. Deleting the App removes all SeatLock data from your device. Because we don't keep your answers on our server, there is nothing for us to delete there.

Children's Privacy

SeatLock is intended for adults preparing for airline pilot interviews and is not directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Security

  • All network requests use HTTPS
  • AI and voice service keys are stored on SeatLock's server, never in the App
  • Data on your device is protected by iOS security features

Changes to This Policy

We'll update this page and the "Last Updated" date when the policy changes. If a change affects what leaves your device, we'll also tell you in the App.
